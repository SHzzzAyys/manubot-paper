# Support Studies

| year | study_id | study_type | cat_scale | design_summary | key_finding | evidence_grade | notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1995 | Dubey1995a | duration_of_immunity | 12 initially infected cats with long follow-up | rechallenge after 39 days and again after 77 months | short-term protection against re-shedding was strong, but 4/9 shed again after 77 months | C | important for long-term durability framing |
| 1995 | DavisDubey1995 | intestinal_stage_mechanism | 12 immune cats and 11 nonimmune cats | compared enteroepithelial development after rechallenge | early intestinal stages were suppressed in immune cats | C | supports intestinal-stage mechanism |
| 1996 | Booth1996 | cryopreservation | non-efficacy development study | developed cryopreservation for T-263 bradyzoites | shows T-263 was once advanced toward product development | C | process study |
| 2002 | MateusPinilla2002 | transmission_modeling | deterministic simulation seeded with 10 to 50 cats and 0 to 75% vaccination coverage | dynamic swine-cat farm model compared field and weaning vaccination schedules while varying cat numbers prevalence and oocyst survival | decreased finishing-pig prevalence was driven mainly by fewer cats (sr2=47%) and shorter oocyst survival (sr2=35%); vaccination still contributed (sr2=5%) | C | supports vaccine utility but shows cat density and environmental persistence remain dominant control levers |
| 2014 | Cornelissen2014 | challenge_model | 5 groups of 3 cats | dose-response challenge model with free bradyzoites and intact cysts plus qPCR | qPCR was more sensitive and several inocula established infection | C | useful for standardized challenge design |
| 2017 | Dubey2017 | T263_enteric_development | 4 cats | observed T-263 enteroepithelial development | partial schizonts and gamonts formed but no oocysts formed | C | explains immunogenic but non-oocyst phenotype |
| 2018 | Zulpo2018 | heterologous_reinfection | 13 cats | tracked homologous and heterologous rechallenge at 12, 24, 36 months | protection waned over time and heterologous reinfection more readily re-induced shedding | C | key for cross-protection and durability |
| 2021 | Schreiber2021 | seroepidemiology_and_antibody_kinetics | 6 experimentally inoculated cats plus 1822 client-owned cats | compared antibody kinetics after oral tissue-cyst versus sporulated-oocyst inoculation and modeled prevalence risk factors in Switzerland | long-term humoral responses persisted; estimated true prevalence was 42.4% overall and 56.3% in outdoor cats vs 22.1% indoors; tissue-cyst and oocyst inoculations had similar early antibody kinetics | C | useful for interpreting serology in vaccine and field surveillance work |
| 2023 | Lykins2023 | sexual_stage_genetics_T263 | mechanistic sequencing work with additional cat-shedding observations | combined structural biology mutant sequencing and cat-passaging observations to dissect T-263 and TgO/GABA-AT | TgO/GABA-AT knockout did not eliminate cat oocyst shedding; sequencing of T-263 linked blocked oocyst formation to conception and zygote-related candidate genes | C | moves the field from phenomenological T-263 protection toward gene-defined transmission-blocking targets |
| 2023 | Zhu2023 | environmental_risk_modeling | systematic dataset of 47 studies with 256 positives among 9635 fecal samples | generalized linear mixed models tested climate and anthropogenic predictors of shedding in free-ranging felids | human population density was positively associated with shedding in domestic cats and wild felids; larger diurnal temperature range increased domestic-cat shedding | C | helps frame where cat-targeted vaccination could have the largest environmental effect |
| 2025 | Zhao2025 | gut_microbiota_during_oocyst_excretion | comparative metagenomics of cats at sexual initiation oocyst excretion and uninfected stages | shotgun metagenomics with KEGG CAZyme virulence-factor and taxonomic analyses | sexual initiation and oocyst excretion reduced bacterial diversity; 39 KEGG pathways 106 CAZymes and 98 virulence factors differed; Bacteroides stercoris emerged as a candidate regulator | C | mechanistic support for microbiota-informed interventions around oocyst excretion |
