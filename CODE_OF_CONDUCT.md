# Code of Conduct

This is a small public research-writing repository. The goal is to keep
discussion professional, evidence-centered, and safe for public collaboration.

## Expected Conduct

- Be precise about evidence, uncertainty, and manuscript scope.
- Critique claims, data extraction, and workflow design without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy boundaries for contact information, local paths, unpublished
  drafts, academic planning notes, and non-public review material.
- Do not add clinical, veterinary, or public-health recommendations that are not
  clearly supported by cited evidence and manuscript scope.
- Avoid unsupported claims about adoption, user counts, institutional backing, or
  production use.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private contact information, private academic drafts, secrets,
  tokens, or local machine paths.
- Misrepresenting the maturity, adoption, provenance, or maintenance status of
  this repository.
- Repeatedly submitting changes that ignore citation, privacy, or evidence
  requirements after review feedback.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, close, hide, or remove issues, pull requests, comments,
or files that violate this code of conduct or the public-data boundary. Serious
privacy or security concerns should be reported through the process in
`SECURITY.md` rather than posted publicly with sensitive details.
